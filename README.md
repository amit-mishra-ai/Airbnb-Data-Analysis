# Airbnb Performance Dashboard

## Project Overview
This project is an interactive Power BI dashboard built to analyze global Airbnb performance across multiple cities. The dashboard focuses on listings growth, market concentration, host performance, booking behavior, pricing trends, customer reviews, and inventory efficiency using data visualization, DAX measures, and business intelligence techniques.

The objective of this project is to uncover actionable business insights from Airbnb data and demonstrate end-to-end data analytics skills including:
- Data Cleaning
- Data Modeling
- DAX Calculations
- KPI Development
- Business Analysis
- Dashboard Design
- Data Storytelling

# Dashboard Features

## 1. Overview Analysis
The overview page highlights:
- Total Listings
- Total Cities
- Total Hosts
- Total Properties
- Total Reviews

It also analyzes Airbnb listing growth trends from 2008–2021 across:
- Entire Place
- Private Room
- Shared Room
- Hotel Room

### Key Insights
- Airbnb experienced rapid expansion between 2010–2015.
- Listings peaked around 2015, showing strong platform growth.
- Growth slowed during 2016–2017, likely due to market saturation and regulatory pressure.
- A short recovery was observed after 2018.
- The sharp decline in 2020 reflects the impact of COVID-19 on travel demand.
- Entire Place listings dominate the platform compared to other property types.

---

# 2. Market Share & Ratings Analysis

This section focuses on:
- City-wise market share
- Superhost distribution
- Average property pricing
- Overall customer ratings

### Key Insights
- Paris, New York, and Sydney together contribute nearly 50% of total listings and reviews.
- Airbnb supply and customer demand are highly concentrated in a few major cities.
- Hotel rooms have the highest average prices, while private rooms are the most affordable.
- Mexico City and Rio de Janeiro have the highest average ratings.
- Hong Kong and Istanbul received comparatively lower ratings.
- Cleanliness and value-for-money metrics appear to be weaker compared to other rating categories.

---

# 3. Host & Booking Intelligence

This page analyzes:
- Superhost performance
- Instant booking behavior
- Response rate analysis
- Dead inventory analysis
- Host tenure performance

### Key Insights

## Superhost Performance
Compared to regular hosts:
- Superhosts charge higher average prices.
- Superhosts maintain significantly better ratings.
- Superhosts provide much faster response times.

This indicates that better service quality allows hosts to charge premium pricing.

---

## Instant Booking Trends
- Bangkok has the highest instant bookable rate.
- New York and Paris show comparatively lower instant booking percentages.
- Cities with higher instant booking availability may improve customer convenience and conversion rates.

---

## Dead Inventory Analysis
Dead inventory represents listings with zero reviews.

### Findings
- Paris has the highest dead inventory despite having the largest active listing base.
- Istanbul shows unusually high zero-review listings relative to active listings.
- Large volumes of inactive listings may indicate:
  - Poor listing visibility
  - Weak customer engagement
  - Oversupply in certain markets

---

## Host Tenure vs Performance
- Hosts with 3–8 years of experience maintain the highest average ratings.
- Mid-tenure hosts also manage the largest listing volumes.
- Very new hosts show lower ratings due to limited operational experience.
- Long-tenure hosts maintain strong ratings but manage fewer listings.
